# Privacy Policy for TUPPEN

Last updated: October 5, 2026

TUPPEN is an offline card game for iPhone.

## Data Collection

TUPPEN does not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

The app does not use:

- user accounts
- advertising
- analytics
- tracking
- third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
- cloud services

## Local Data

Game progress, settings, and statistics are stored locally on the user's device.

This information is used only to provide the app's functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Removing the app may also remove locally stored app data, subject to the behavior of iOS and device backups.

## Network Access

TUPPEN does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

## Children

TUPPEN does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If TUPPEN's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please use the contact information provided on the TUPPEN support page.
